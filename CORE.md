# Lake Champlain Pools, Spas & Stoves

# Business System Modernization Context

## Vision

Replace Evosus with a modern, cloud-based, user-friendly operating
platform that simplifies operations, improves the customer experience,
reduces manual work, and supports AI-powered workflows.

## Business Volume (Busy Season)

-   600--900 retail transactions/week
-   15--20 deliveries, installations, or pool builds/week
-   50--80 service calls/week

## Current Challenges

### Customer Management

-   Customer lookup requires exact information; want Google-like fuzzy
    search and predictive typing.
-   Customer names/addresses require updates in multiple places.
-   Address auto-complete and ZIP-to-city/state missing.
-   Poor handling of multiple properties, contacts, and billing/service
    addresses.
-   No easy way to distinguish which property is being serviced.

### Customer Communication

-   Communication is fragmented across:
    -   Water testing
    -   Phone calls
    -   Text messages (Podium)
    -   Email
    -   Internal notes
-   No unified customer timeline.

### Photos & Documentation

-   Photo uploads are antiquated.
-   Photos must be downloaded and manually labeled.
-   Difficult to review historical job photos.
-   Technicians rarely capture equipment/backyard layouts because the
    process is cumbersome.
-   Would like customer property profiles with:
    -   Equipment photos
    -   Access instructions
    -   Gate codes
    -   Pet information
    -   Electrical/water locations
    -   Historical service photos

### Inventory

-   Difficult to change SKUs.
-   Inventory maintenance is unintuitive.
-   Manual reorder points and ordering levels.
-   Difficult to know which large-ticket items (e.g. spas by
    model/color) are in stock.
-   Inventory transfers between store and truck are confusing and affect
    invoicing.
-   Cannot add internal notes to inventory items (example: "These steps
    require a mat underneath.")

### Purchasing

-   Cannot efficiently purchase/report across multiple vendors.
-   Manual ordering process.
-   No intelligent purchasing recommendations.

### Service & Dispatch

-   Can schedule jobs before required parts have been received.
-   Cannot block overbooked service days.
-   Notes are difficult for technicians to find.
-   No real-time technician GPS or status.
-   No route optimization.

### Sales / POS

-   Quotes are confusing for customers.
-   Difficult to move from Sales Order/Service Order to POS.
-   Cannot combine payments easily.
-   Cannot automatically apply credit card processing fees.

### Workflow

-   Too many employee handoffs.
-   Notes live in multiple places.
-   Easy for tasks to fall through the cracks.
-   Multiple workarounds using Google Docs/Sheets.

### Reliability & Support

-   Runtime errors.
-   Antiquated interface.
-   Limited cloud functionality.
-   Training resources are poor.
-   Vendor support is difficult.

## Current Supplemental Software

-   Google Docs
-   Google Sheets
-   Podium (texting/calling)
-   Fleet tracking

## Desired Future State

### Customer Experience

-   One customer record.
-   One communication timeline.
-   Fast Google-style search.
-   Multiple properties and contacts.
-   Address validation/autocomplete.

### Inventory

-   Barcode-first.
-   Real-time inventory.
-   Easy bulk edits.
-   Automated reorder suggestions.
-   Seasonal forecasting.
-   Internal product notes.
-   Large-item visibility.

### Service

-   Mobile technician app.
-   Photo uploads from the field.
-   GPS tracking.
-   Capacity management.
-   Route optimization.
-   Parts availability validation before scheduling.

### Payments & Sales

-   Cleaner customer-facing quotes.
-   Unified payment workflow.
-   Credit card surcharge support (where permitted).

### AI Opportunities

-   Natural language search.
-   Purchase recommendations.
-   Inventory forecasting.
-   Executive dashboards.
-   Suggested upsells.
-   Customer/property summaries.

## Non-Negotiables

-   Cloud-native
-   Excellent POS
-   Strong inventory management
-   Integrated CRM
-   Service scheduling
-   Mobile apps
-   Open API
-   AI-ready
-   Easy employee training
-   Excellent vendor support

## Guiding Principle

**One customer. One timeline. One workflow.** The software should adapt
to our business---not force our business to adapt to the software.
